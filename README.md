 Sales Performance for 2014-2017

 Overview:

 Transformed raw sales data into actionable insights. This Excel dashboard provides a high-level view of monthly sales, profit margins, and customer demographics using 
 Pivot Tables for data aggregation, and interactive charts to visualize Year-over-Year (YoY) growth and sales representative efficiency.

 Business Impact:
 
 Identifies high-performing regions and products.
 Profit margines alongside Sales volume to ensure high revenue.
 Top contributing products and categories helping inventry to focus on high margine items.

 Key Performance Indicators:
 
Total Sales: ₹22,97,201

Total Profit: ₹2,86,397

Average Profit Margin: 12%

Quantity Sold: 37,873 units

Visual Highlights:

Pie chart - Shipment wise sales.

Bar charts - Total sales by Region, Profit Trend by Category, Top 10 products with hightest profit margin.

Line chart - Discount rate vs profit margin.

Tool Used:

Microsoft Excel (Pivot tables, Slicers, Charts, Filters)

Insight Summary:

Revenue Growth: The business achieved a total revenue of ₹22,97,201, with a steady upward trend observed from 2014 to 2017.

Profitability Core: While the overall profit is ₹2,86,397, the average profit margin stands at 12%. This indicates a healthy baseline, though high-discount periods show a direct correlation with margin dips.

Shipping Preferences: The majority of customers opt for standard shipping modes (59%), providing an opportunity to negotiate better rates with those specific logistics providers.

Product Performance: The "Top 10 Products" analysis reveals that a small percentage of inventory drives the bulk of the profit.

Discount Impact: Higher discounts correlate with lower profit margins.
